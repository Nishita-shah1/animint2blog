Week 4

d3.geo.path, coverage, and flaky tooltips

2026-06-15 – 2026-06-21

Outcome

Two separate problems showed up on PR #328: Codecov still disliked the new JS, and some tooltip tests passed only some of the time.

Context

On 17 June I computed a planar SVG path string as a fallback, then preferred d3.geo.path when it existed. The idea was to execute both branches so coverage would rise without changing the picture. Toby asked why coverage would change, what a planar SVG path was, and why d3.geo.path would ever be missing. The honest answer was that I did not know how to pass Codecov and was guessing. He told me to ignore Codecov and use d3.geo.path.

What I did

  • Added Sys.sleep(0.3) in helper-function.R because the browser sometimes painted tooltipID after the assertion ran.
  • Dropped the dual-path coverage hack after review.
  • Started rewriting the renderer to d3.geo.path only.

Learnings

Coverage is not a reason to keep dead branches. If d3 is always present in the animint page, the fallback is noise. Flaky interactive tests are often timing, not geometry.

Finish the d3.geo.path implementation, then start issue #258: move getCommonChunk() to C++.
